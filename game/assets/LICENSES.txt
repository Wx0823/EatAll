EatAll v0.1 assets and dependencies

The original EatAll character, terrain, UI and sound effects are drawn/generated
at runtime by this project's code. They are not extracted from the reference video.
AI-generated concept images in design/art/concepts are design references and are
not bundled as runtime game assets.
